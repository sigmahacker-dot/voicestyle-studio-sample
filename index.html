The Studio

A heritage shopfront that was made for music

Lessons are held at the historic “Bootstore Voice Studio” on Bridge Street, Kensington — look for the S. Heanes Boot Store signage on the side of the building.

Easy to find, easy to settle in

Bridge Street is accessible from either Kensington Road or The Parade, right in the heart of Kensington. While lessons are on, parents can make a tea or coffee in the waiting area, visit the hotel across the road with its open fireplaces, or take siblings to Borthwick Park behind the studio.

Not everyone will become a professional singer — but you will be surprised what talent can be uncovered during lessons. It is also a great way to build confidence and simply have fun.

  • One-off trial lesson before you commit to a term
  • Weekly lessons only — steady progress, week by week
  • Start mid-term and your fee is adjusted to your start date
  • Waiting list available when your preferred time is full

Lessons & Fees

Simple term pricing, no surprises

Lessons are a weekly term commitment based on public school terms (usually 10 weeks). Pay in full at the start of term, or in two halves at weeks 1 and 6.

Private lessons 30 min

One-on-one coaching shaped around your voice, your songs and your goals — technique, confidence and repertoire, at your pace.

$55 per lesson with Sue · $50 with Neve or Darcy

Duo lessons 30 min

Learn with a friend, sibling or partner. Two voices, one lesson — a fun, affordable way to start singing together.

$30 each per lesson with Sue

Group sessions 45 min

Harmony work and group singing — one of Sue's specialties. Organise your own group, or register and we'll match you into one.

$30 each per session with Sue

Performances

Two concerts a year — and a stage that feels safe

We hold two student performances each year, one mid-year and one at the end of the year. Performing is encouraged, never forced.

  • Nervous? Your teacher, or another singer or two, can join you on stage
  • A real goal to work towards each term
  • Family and friends welcome
